SoundStorm is a C++ library for sound and music, used in various VoxelStorm games - most notably AdvertCity and sphereFACE.

Unique features include spacial sound with natural doppler shift through speed of sound simulation, including head-related transfer function (HRTF) including head shadowing and time delay, for fully realistic immersive sound in VR applications, and the capability to smoothly crossfade across multiple music tracks on demand.
